Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our air cooler repair, maintenance, and doorstep services in Hyderabad & Secunderabad.

What types and brands of air coolers do you repair in Hyderabad?

We repair all air cooler models including desert coolers, personal room coolers, tower coolers, window coolers, and commercial duct coolers. We service all major brands such as Symphony, Kenstar, Bajaj, Crompton, Havells, Voltas, Orient, Usha, Hindware, and McCoy with genuine spare parts.

How quickly can an air cooler technician arrive at my doorstep?

Our certified doorstep technicians arrive within 90 minutes anywhere across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, with same-day fixes guaranteed for 99% of repair requests.

Why is my cooler fan humming or not spinning?

A humming fan that won't spin is usually due to a failed capacitor, jammed motor bushings/bearings, or a burnt motor coil. Our technician carries genuine replacement capacitors, lubricants, and copper-wound motors to fix this on the spot.

When should cooler honeycomb cooling pads be replaced?

Due to hard borewell water in Hyderabad, honeycomb pads accumulate calcium and salt scale. We recommend replacing them every 1 to 2 seasons to restore ice-cold airflow, prevent stale odors, and boost air delivery by up to 35%.

What is included in seasonal cooler deep servicing?

Our deep servicing includes complete water tank draining and anti-bacterial cleaning, water distributor channel descaling, submersible pump inspection, motor bushing lubrication, blade balancing, and an 18-point electrical safety check.

Do you provide a warranty on air cooler repair and parts?

Yes! All cooler repair services and genuine replacement parts installed by CoolerWorks come with a 1-year service warranty for your complete peace of mind.
